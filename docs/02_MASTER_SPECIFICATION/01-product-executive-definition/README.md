## Purpose

What is known and confirmed about the platform at the executive level — the
product's identity, purpose, and governing constraints in language a decision-maker
can read in under 10 minutes.

## What belongs here

- Platform identity: what this product is and what it definitively is not
- Phase 1 summary (informational site + governed CMS, not CRM/ERP/portal)
- The 10 source facts that cannot be contradicted (from `AGENT.md` §1)
- Key constraints and the rationale for them
- Summary of what is authorized to build vs. what is blocked

## What does NOT belong here

- Detailed functional requirements (→ Ch.08)
- Architecture decisions (→ Ch.21–23)
- Open questions list (→ Ch.40 + `03_GOVERNANCE/open-questions/`)
