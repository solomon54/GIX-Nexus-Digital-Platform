### 1. Company Identity & Ownership

- **Official Name:** GIX Nexus Telecom and Power. [Page 1]
- **Legal/Ownership Status:** Ethiopian-owned. [Page 1]
- **Industry Classification:** Telecommunications and power engineering company. [Page 1]
- **Core Promise:** Delivering reliable, innovative, and cost-effective infrastructure solutions. [Page 1]

### 2. Organizational Roles & Leadership

- **Managing Director:** Getachew Teshome. [Page 10]
- **Leadership Qualifications:** Practical experience in satellite communications (SATCOM), including installation, operation, preventive and corrective maintenance of satellite systems; understanding of RF systems and VSAT technology. [Page 2]
- **Reported Organizational Structure:** [Page 8]
  - Managing Director
  - General Manager
  - Project Engineers
  - Administrative and Finance
  - Field Supervisors
  - Lineman
  - OPS (Operations) Technician
- **Personnel Qualifications:** Mention of "qualified personnel, including a Cisco Certified professional"; Fiber Optic Technician; OSP (Outside Plant) Technician; Telecommunications Technician; HSEQ Representative. [Page 2, 8]

### 3. Service Domains & Capabilities

- **Domain Count:** Stated as "7+ Service Domains". [Page 3]
- **Service Scope:** Site surveys, installation, testing, commissioning, maintenance, and 24/7 technical support. [Page 1, 3, 5]
- **Key Capability Groupings:** [Page 5]
  1.  Telecommunications Infrastructure (Outdoor & Indoor)
  2.  Fiber Optic Solutions
  3.  Satellite & Wireless Communications
  4.  Network Infrastructure
  5.  Telecom Power Systems
  6.  Maintenance & Technical Support
- **Specific Specialized Services:** [Page 6, 7]
  - BTS/NodeB/eNodeB/5G site installation support.
  - VSAT antenna installation and dish alignment.
  - Microwave & Radio communication (point-to-point/multipoint).
  - DC power systems, rectifiers, and battery banks.
  - Structured Cabling (Cat6/Cat6A/Cat7).
  - Indoor Mobile Coverage (DAS, small cell).
  - Security & Low Voltage Systems (CCTV, Access Control, BMS).

### 4. Standards, Testing & Quality

- **Engineering Standards:** Commitment to "international engineering standards" and "industry best practices." [Page 3, 4]
- **Specific Testing Mentioned:** "Fluke testing and certification" (referring to network outlet/cabling installation). [Page 7]
- **Equipment Quality:** Uses specialized tools and test instruments (e.g., OTDR testing, Digital Multimeters, Network Cable Testers). [Page 6, 9]

### 5. HSEQ & Safety Performance Statements

- **Policy Name:** "HSEQ Policy" (Health, Safety, Environment, and Quality). [Page 8]
- **Safety Objective:** "Zero Accident Objective." [Page 8]
- **Performance Statement:** "100% Safety-first approach on every project." [Page 3]
- **Commitment:** Safety is "our highest priority." [Page 8]

### 6. Operational & Geographic Context

- **Current Operational Area:** "Across Ethiopia." [Page 1]
- **Headquarters:** Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. [Page 10]
- **Service Availability:** "24/7 technical support" and "Round-the-clock maintenance and fault response." [Page 1, 3]

### 7. Client & Procurement Context

- **Target Clients:** Lists 14 categories including Telecom Operators, Government Ministries, International Organizations/UN Agencies, NGOs, Banks, and Data Centers. [Page 9]
- **Engagement Model:** Works as a contractor or subcontractor. [Page 9]
- **Procurement Status:** "Vendor Registration Ready." [Page 1]

### B. RESTRICTED / NON-ESTABLISHED CLAIMS

The following claims **must not be made** in the digital platform specification without further evidence, as they are not established by the source:

1.  **Company-Level ISO Certification:** Page 10 explicitly lists achieving industry certifications as a **future objective**.
2.  **Specific Named Clients:** The source lists "Target Clients," not existing/past clients. No specific organization names are confirmed as customers.
3.  **Employee Headcount:** No specific number of staff is provided.
4.  **Operational Presence outside Ethiopia:** Expansion to the "East African region" is a **future objective**.
